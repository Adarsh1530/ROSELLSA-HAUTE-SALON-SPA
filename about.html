About Rosellsa

Where Parisian Haute Elegance Meets Timeless Moroccan Healing & Clinical Precision

Rosellsa is a premier boutique beauty sanctuary nestled in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. Conceived as a peaceful haven away from the rapid pace of the city, our sanctuary blends the refined sophistication of French high-touch aesthetics with the deeply grounding traditions of Moroccan bath rituals.

Come in, relax, and let's talk about your hair, skin, and wellbeing. Together, our master artisans work with you to understand your personal goals and curate a tailored therapy. We believe beauty is not an imposition of trends, but the careful elevation of your own natural elegance.

We exclusively formulate with prestigious French marine botanical skincare (Thalgo Paris), pure Moroccan eucalyptus olive soaps, organic botanical sugaring pastes, and Italian keratin hair therapies. Our sanctuary adheres to hospital-grade sterilization protocols, cruelty-free sourcing, and low-tox holistic wellbeing.
