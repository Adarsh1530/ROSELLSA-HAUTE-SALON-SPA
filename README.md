# ROSELLSA HAUTE BEAUTY SANCTUARY &bull; MUSCAT

Official website for **Rosellsa Haute Beauty Sanctuary & Salon**, located in Al Khuwair, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman.

## Features & Pages

- **Home Sanctuary Portal (`index.html` / `home.html`):** Editorial hero section, signature rituals showcase, lookbook, and direct concierge callout.
- **Philosophy & Heritage (`about.html`):** Westfolk-inspired architectural elegance, team background, and sanctuary values.
- **Service Catalog (`services.html`):** 52 categorized bespoke treatments across Hair Couture, Clinical Skincare, Hands & Feet, Royal Hammam & Spa, and Bridal.
- **Certified Mobile Team (`home-services.html`):** VIP at-home salon & wellness services across Muscat Governorates.
- **Retail Collection (`products.html`):** Curated luxury haircare, clinical skincare, and wellness products with direct WhatsApp ordering.
- **Client Reviews & Feedback (`feedback.html`):** Client testimonials and interactive feedback submission form.
- **Specialist Diagnostics (`consultation.html`):** 1-on-1 micro-camera scalp diagnostic, dermal radiance assessment, and bridal preview booking.
- **Direct VIP Booking Engine (`book-now.html`):** Complete booking wizard with service selection, date & time slots, client notes, and instant confirmation.
- **Slide-Over Booking Drawer:** Universal slide-over booking drawer available from every page.
- **Sanctuary Contacts & Location (`contact.html`):** Al Khuwair villa address, Google Maps route, opening hours, and direct concierge links.

## Tech Stack

- **Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Zero build steps required** — ultra-fast static loading
- **Responsive design** optimized for all desktop, tablet, and mobile devices (tested for iOS Safari & Android)
- **Currency:** Official OMR pricing throughout

## Deployment Instructions (Vercel)

This repository is ready for one-click deployment on [Vercel](https://vercel.com):

1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Click **Add New...** &rarr; **Project**.
3. Under **Import Git Repository**, select **`Adarsh1530/ROSELLSA-HAUTE-SALON-SPA`**.
4. Configure Project settings:
   - **Framework Preset:** `Other` (Static HTML/CSS/JS)
   - **Root Directory:** `./`
   - **Build Command:** Leave empty
   - **Output Directory:** Leave empty
5. Click **Deploy**.
6. Your website will be live worldwide on a fast, secure HTTPS URL within seconds!
